# Deadlock match analyser
Will help you understand why you lost/won a game, the deciding factors such as objectives or someone's death.

It's a lightweight app, you don't need anything special installed and doesn't require any login, you just drop your replay file in the app and it will give you an overview of your game !!

Made just for funsies so some wording/logic might be clunky but I'm just happy it works somehow someway :p
